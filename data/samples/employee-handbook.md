# AcmeCorp Employee Handbook

## Welcome

Welcome to AcmeCorp. This handbook summarizes the policies, benefits, and
expectations that apply to every employee. It is a living document and is
reviewed each quarter by the People Operations team.

## Employment Basics

### Probation Period

New hires serve a 90-day probation period. During probation, either party may
end the employment relationship with one week of notice. Performance is reviewed
at the 60-day mark with the direct manager.

### Work Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour
lunch break. Flexible arrangements are available for roles approved by the
department head.

### Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their
probation period. Remote days must be logged in the internal scheduling tool at
least 48 hours in advance.

## Compensation and Benefits

### Salary Review Cycle

Salaries are reviewed annually in January. Reviews consider performance ratings,
market benchmarks, and company budget. Promotion salary increases are applied at
the start of the following month.

### Annual Leave

Employees accrue 25 working days of annual leave per year. Unused days carry over
up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year. Leave requests must be
approved by the direct manager at least two weeks in advance.

### Health Insurance

Comprehensive health insurance covers the employee, a spouse, and dependent
children. Dental and vision coverage are optional add-ons purchased through
payroll deduction.

## Conduct and Culture

### Code of Conduct

AcmeCorp expects honesty, respect, and integrity in every interaction. Retaliation
against anyone who reports misconduct in good faith is a serious disciplinary
offense.

### Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may accept gifts with a value below 50 USD. Any gift above this limit,
or from a current supplier under active negotiation, must be declined or declared
to compliance.

## Leaving AcmeCorp

Resignations require a notice period of 30 days for individual contributors and
60 days for managers. A structured exit interview is offered to every departing
employee, and all company equipment must be returned on the final day.
